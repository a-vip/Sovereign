Avi Perera is a British academic, international lawyer, and AI governance researcher whose work spans the University of Cambridge and international disarmament and AI policy contexts. His research examines how emerging technologies — from frontier AI models and algorithmic surveillance to lethal autonomous weapons systems (LAWS) — challenge international humanitarian law, accountability, and democratic oversight.

⬡ Operating Principles

Built for Rigor. Never for Clicks.

01 // Primary-Source Provenance

Every Signal Traces to the Source

Whether it's an EU AI Act statutory clause, a data center registry entry, or a live field incident, Sovereign attaches timestamped provenance and direct primary links so analysts can audit every claim in one click.

02 // Zero-Hallucination RAG AI

Citation-Locked Intelligence Search

Ask SovDash AI retrieves directly from Sovereign's curated database, legal profiles, and live event corpus rather than guessing from open web noise — turning hours of cross-referencing into verified answers in seconds.

03 // True Independence

No Ads. No Surveillance. No Agenda.

Sovereign never sells user attention, never runs behavioral ad trackers, and never buries public-interest research behind opaque paywalls. Funding comes transparently from Pro users, supporters, grants, and enterprise builds.

04 // Public Impact & Enterprise Scale

One Engine, Two Missions

The exact spatial engine and RAG AI architecture that powers our public observatory also deploys privately for enterprise teams — enabling commercial partnerships to cross-subsidize free global public access.
