⬡ Operation Profile & Public-Interest Mission

About the
Observatory.

Sovereign is an independent, open-methodology AI safety, policy, and geopolitical intelligence observatory—uniting live 3D/2D geospatial monitoring, 60-jurisdiction statutory tracking, Epoch AI compute telemetry, and Ask SovDash AI.

01 / Public-Interest Mission & Why It Matters

Sovereign was founded to close a critical accountability blindspot: frontier AI compute clusters now exceed 15.12 GW across 96 tracked megaclusters, yet 87.6% of that power operates outside comprehensive national AI law. Meanwhile, autonomous targeting, algorithmic immigration surveillance, biometric welfare exclusion, and chatbot sycophancy harms unfold daily with limited cross-border visibility.

We map global AI legislation, physical compute infrastructure, 5 AI Ethics & Society layers, and live breaking signals on both a 3D Cesium Globe and a distortion-free Equal Earth 2D projection—paired with Ask SovDash AI, a grounded research copilot audited for zero phantom citations.

Our core public datasets, Whitepaper v2.0, and GeoJSON APIs are published openly under CC-BY-4.0 so researchers, policymakers, journalists, and citizens everywhere share the same verified ground truth.
